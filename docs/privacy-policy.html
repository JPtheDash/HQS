Privacy Policy

Hanuman: Quest for Sanjeevini · Developer: Dijytal
Last updated: 28 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Hanuman: Quest for Sanjeevini (“the App”, package com.dijytal.hanumanquestforsanjeevini) handles information. We designed the App to respect your privacy: it is a single-player, offline game that does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The App does not ask for, collect, or send any personal or sensitive user or device data. Specifically, the App does not:

  • require an account, sign-in, name, email, or phone number;
  • collect your location, contacts, photos, files, or device identifiers;
  • use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking software;
  • send any data to us or to any third party over the internet.

Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves your game progress (such as coins collected and the level you have reached) locally on your device using standard on-device storage. This information never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is removed if you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App.

Permissions

The App’s technical framework declares the standard network (INTERNET) permission that ships with the app platform. The App itself does not use it to send or receive personal data; gameplay runs entirely offline.

Children’s privacy

The App is suitable for a general audience, including children. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. We do not knowingly gather data from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

Third-party services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or social services. If you download the App from a store such as Google Play, that store’s own privacy policy governs any data the store itself collects during download and installation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
